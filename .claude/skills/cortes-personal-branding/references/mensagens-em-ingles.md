# Mensagens em inglês para clientes e prospects

Modelos para adaptar. O tom é o do mercado americano de criadores: direto, simpático, confiante, curto. Sempre entregue a tradução em português abaixo da mensagem final.

## Índice
1. Regras de tom
2. Prospecção (primeiro contato / DM fria)
3. Envio de amostra
4. Proposta e preço
5. Entrega de vídeos

7. Cobrança e follow-up
8. Frases úteis do dia a dia

## 1. Regras de tom

- Curto vence longo. DM fria: até ~5 linhas.
- Fale do cliente, não de você: "your podcast", "your audience", não "I am a video editor with 5 years…".
- Seja específico: cite o episódio, um momento, um número.
- Sem "Dear", "Kindly", "I hope this message finds you well", "Sorry to bother you". Soa robótico ou inseguro.
- Use contrações (I'm, you're, I've): soa natural.
- Uma pergunta ou próximo passo claro no final.

Troque o que soa "traduzido do português":
| Evite | Prefira |
|---|---|
| "I would like to offer my services" | "I'd love to help with your short-form content" |
| "Please let me know your opinion" | "Let me know what you think" |
| "I stay at your disposal" | "Happy to answer any questions" |
| "Kindly send the payment" | "Here's the invoice — let me know if you need anything else" |
| "I made a video of you" | "I cut a few clips from your latest episode" |

## 2. Prospecção (primeiro contato / DM fria)

> Hey [Name] — loved your episode with [Host/Guest], especially the part about [tema específico].
> I edit short-form clips for personal brands and pulled [3] moments from it that I think would do really well as Reels.
> Want me to send them over? No strings attached.

## 3. Envio de amostra

> Here you go: [LINK]
> I picked these because [motivo curto — ex.: "the hook on #1 is a strong contrarian take your audience will react to"].
> If you like them, I can turn every episode into [X] clips a week. Happy to jump on a quick call.

## 4. Proposta e preço

> Here's what I'd suggest:
> - [X] short clips per week from your podcast/lives
> - Hook, captions, b-roll and on-screen CTA included
> - [X] rounds of revisions
> - Delivered within [X] days of each episode
> Investment: [PRICE]/month. We can start with a [2-week] trial if you'd like to see how it goes.

## 5. Entrega de vídeos

> Hey [Name], this week's clips are ready: [LINK]
> Quick notes: #[N] is my favorite — [motivo]. Suggested captions are in the doc.
> Let me know if you'd like any changes.

## 6. Pedido de feedback e revisão

> How did the last batch perform? Any clips you'd like more (or less) of?
> Totally open to feedback — the more I know about your audience, the better the cuts get.

Resposta a pedido de ajuste:
> Got it, thanks! I'll update [the hook on #2 / the captions] and send the new version by [DAY].

## 7. Cobrança e follow-up

Follow-up de prospecção (3–5 dias depois, sem resposta):
> Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send the clips over if you're curious.

Cobrança gentil:
> Hi [Name], quick reminder that the invoice for [period] is due [DATE]: [LINK]. Thanks!

Cobrança atrasada:
> Hi [Name], the invoice from [DATE] is still open. Could you take a look when you get a chance? Let me know if there's any issue on your end.

## 8. Frases úteis do dia a dia

- "Sounds good!" — Combinado!
- "On it." — Já estou fazendo.
- "Quick question:" — Uma dúvida rápida:
- "Just to make sure I got it right…" — Só pra confirmar se entendi…
- "I'll have it to you by Friday." — Te entrego até sexta.
- "No worries at all." — Imagina, sem problema.
- "Let's hop on a quick call." — Vamos fazer uma call rápida.
- "That's a great point." — Ótimo ponto.
- "Looking forward to working together." — Animado pra trabalharmos juntos.
